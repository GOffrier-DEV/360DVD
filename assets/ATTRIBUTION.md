# DVD-Video logo artwork

- Artwork: DVD-Video Logo
- Author/source attribution: DVD Format / Logo Licensing Company
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DVD-Video_Logo.svg
- The source SVG is available from the page above. `dvd-video-logo.png` is a
  transparent raster rendering of that SVG; `dvd-video-logo.bmp` is a white
  alpha-mask texture derived from the PNG for the Xbox 360 logo renderer.
- The SVG is identified on Wikimedia Commons as public domain due to simple
  geometric shapes/text, but may still be subject to trademark restrictions.

## Compact Disc logo

- Artwork: Compact Disc wordmark used in `DVD-plus-CD-original.png`.
- Author: Sony and Philips.
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Compact_Disc_wordmark.svg
- Wikimedia Commons identifies the wordmark as public domain because it
  consists of simple shapes/text below the copyright threshold. It may still
  be subject to trademark restrictions.

## 3D compact disc model

- Model: Compact Disc, by [SEMA Game Studio](https://sketchfab.com/sema.game.studio).
- Source page: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/compact-disc-06254ae4c6c04bc4b1cb5bd9b6c2ea41
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- The model and its texture are converted for use by the Xbox 360 CD scene as
  `compact-disc.bin` and `compact-disc.png`.

## Xbox 360 Blades resources

`Blades/` contains adapted Xbox 360 dashboard DVD layouts and original
icons, plus the Xenon font from the installed SDK. These are Microsoft
assets and are not covered by the DVD logo's attribution or the project's
GPL-2.0-or-later source-code license (see [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE)).
