# DVD-Video logo artwork

- Artwork: DVD-Video Logo
- Author/source attribution: DVD Format / Logo Licensing Company
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DVD-Video_Logo.svg
- The source SVG is available from the page above. `dvd-video-logo.png` is a
  transparent raster rendering of that SVG; `dvd-video-logo.bmp` is a white
  alpha-mask texture derived from the PNG for the Xbox 360 rendering test.
- The SVG is identified on Wikimedia Commons as public domain due to simple
  geometric shapes/text, but may still be subject to trademark restrictions.
